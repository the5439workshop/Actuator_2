Here are the files for the acuator, I was planing to release them sooner, but things got in the way. These are as is and not very well documented. You will need to adjust tolerances if you try to manifacture them as it will be specific to how well you CNC cuts. I cut the gears from 7075 aluminium, which is typically not a great material for gears, but I dont have the tools to cut steal so it is what it is. The rest is in 6061 alu or 3d printed in petg. It works, but dont expect longevity comparible to what you can buy. Economically, you are better of buying actuators, learning wise you are better of designing your own. So, not sure who this is for. I will update the files, once I have gotten further with the project. For me it is about designing and manifacturing all the core components, becaouse that's how I enjoy learning. These acutators are not designed for any water or dusty enviorments, the cooling system, will bring all particles through the actuator. Ideally, you want the stator on the outside for cooling, but I don't want to hand wire 10 of those stators, so compromises have been made. 

This is not a guide for how to build them, just the collection of the files. At somepoint I might make a guide... 

There are 5 types bearings:

 * 55x45x6 mm
 * 44x35x5 mm
 * 21x15x4 mm
 * 12x8x3.5 mm
 * 8x4x3 mm x6
